# Harbor Heat

A coastal city adventure in Mainsail: courier parcels, street crews, four race circuits, ground vehicles and aircraft. It consumes published `@jgengine/*` packages.

Run `bun run dev`, `bun run check-types` and `bun run test` from this directory. `bun run vite build` produces the standalone build. Provision the seven licensed asset sources declared in `src/game/assets.ts` with the published assets CLI before launching a fresh checkout; the original models are bundled from `src/art/`.

The teal dispatch booth is beside the starting boardwalk. Press **E / Use** or tap the prompt to take a standard parcel and start a timed delivery. The goal card shows the distance, an arrow toward the drop and the clock, which turns coral near the end; the minimap marks the same parcel tower. Stop within the handoff radius, then press **E / Use**. Boardwalk breakfast, Plaza night shift and Sunset spare parts rotate after successful runs. Delivery pays cash, a remaining-time bonus and 15 cred. Missing a deadline returns the parcel without a cash penalty; clinic and police recovery end an active delivery.

Use **WASD** to move or drive, **Shift** to sprint, **E** to interact and **F** to exit a vehicle. **P** or the pause button stops the world and delivery clock. Settings retain their own pause reason; closing settings opened from pause returns to the pause menu. The pause menu can save progress. The receipt offers Try again, Free roam or Title, and Title saves first. Reload returns to the title; Continue restores campaign progress and an active delivery with its saved remaining time. Existing legacy save identifiers remain supported.

Garage purchases use the published SDK's entity allocator: each paid vehicle has a distinct identity, even when buying the same model at one game time or after reload. Existing saved vehicle identities remain intact.

The dispatch booth, three parcel towers, coconut palm, Islander bus, HHPD light bar and title poster are original repository art. The readable generator is `scripts/author-harbor.mjs`; run it with Node to rebuild the GLBs. See [art credits](src/art/README.md) and the repository's [content provenance](../CONTENT-PROVENANCE.md) for source attribution. Existing KayKit, Quaternius and ambientCG credits and licenses remain in force.

## Design checklist

Read from `src/game/city.ts` (the world: people, places, factions, jobs, reactions), `src/game/{jobs,progression,entities,handroll}`. Numbers are what the build does now.

- **Core loop.** On foot or in a vehicle, take work in Mainsail: the eight-job story from "Welcome to the Isle" through Front Money and Protection to Sal (offered in person or on the phone, `T`), side jobs (The Ledger, the Ocean Loop, Take the Marina) and Dee Kalani's repeatable job, courier runs from the teal dispatch booth, taxi fares with the taxi light on (`Y` in any car), flower runs from Boardwalk Blooms (`E` at the counter), street races for a $200 stake (phone `T` → Side work, or paged after 20:00), bounties after Dock Sweep, four race circuits and the hidden stashes. Cash pays for weapons, gear and the garage; cred levels gate the good stock.
- **Progression — three systems.**
  - [x] Cred 1–10 at 150 + 130 × level, gating the Carmine SMG (2), Boardwalk shotgun (3) and Cicada GT (4); missions pay 100–800, a bounty 140, a race 150, a stash 25.
  - [x] Garage. Pico 800 → Bandolero 2,400 → Vagabond 3,800 → Cicada GT 6,500 → Islander bus 9,000, then aircraft from the Maverick 44 at 12,000 to the Harrier V at 110,000. Each car's mass, grip and centre of mass are its own numbers, so the ladder changes handling and not just top speed.
  - [x] Contracts. Three courier routes (250 at 65 s, 400 at 95 s, 500 at 110 s, then rotating), a rush variant at 35% of the deadline for a $60 bond, bounties at 350 + 175 per completion up to eight, and 6,000 for bagging every stash.
  - [x] Gigs. Taxi fares at 60 + $0.80/m (150–450), with a deadline at 45 km/h + 15 s, +$1 per second left, a tip up to 25% that crashes (-20 mood), dents and wanted stars (-4 a star per second) eat, and +10% a fare in a streak (up to 50%, 60 s between fares); 10 cred a fare. Flower runs: three drops at 120 + $0.35/m (up to 600), bruised above 60 km/h, tip up to 30%; 15 cred. Street races: $200 stake against two rivals over one of the four circuits, first pays $600 and 40 cred, second $200. Leaving the car loses a fare; dying or arrest drops any gig.
- **Turf, fronts and heat feed each other.**
  - [x] Turf. Port Carmine, Palm Heights, Canal Row and the Marina start Carmine-held, each with a hold (60, 80, 50, 40) that drifts back to 60. A Carmine soldier dropped on their block takes 25 off it and reports 30 heat; at 0 the block is yours. Their corners carry 1–3 soldiers by hold, one more after dark, who only turn on you once the Carmines' mood is -20 or worse; dropped crews return after 45 s.
  - [x] Pushback. Once the Carmines are at -20, every 83–92 s each block you hold has a 70% chance of a crew push (-15 hold, three soldiers if you are within 150 m), and a raid lost on a front takes 10 more; at 0 they take it back with a hold of 40. The war runs on the engine's `defineTurfWar`. Your blocks pay $0.50/s each into the fronts' safe, and residents there never report you to HHPD.
  - [x] The Riptides. A second crew, a smuggling ring under Inez Corvo, holds the Saltworks Yards (hold 70), the East Strand (50) and the Low Flats airstrip (45). Rising Tide (Marco, after the dock sweep, $1,800) sinks their four-man boat crew on the Strand, sends them to -25 mood and starts a crew war: from then on each crew pushes into the other's neighbouring blocks every 109–117 s at 50% (-12 hold, two soldiers a side fighting it out if you are within 150 m), and a block flips to whichever crew wore it to 0. Soldiers one crew drops for the other cost you no heat. Angry Riptides (-20 or worse) push into your blocks next to theirs every 91 s at 60%. Inez then offers Corvo's Offer ($2,400: clear four Carmines off Canal Row), and Marco offers Take the Low Flats ($2,000). The phone shows each block's owner and who is pushing it.
  - [x] Fronts. Neon Lounge $3,000 ($3/s, +$3/s at night), Boardwalk Arcade $2,200 ($2/s +1), Pier Three car wash $4,500 ($4/s, needs the Port), Villa Row motel $6,500 ($5/s +2, needs Palm Heights). They pay into one safe you empty in person, and stop paying above two stars. Nobody sells to you at two stars or on a Carmine block.
  - [x] Raids. Every 60 s, 60% chance the Carmines hit a standing front: three soldiers and a 60 s clock. Drop them all for +10 hold and -4 Carmine mood; let the clock run out and half the safe walks, the front is wrecked and reopening costs 30% of its price. The Protection job stages one.
  - [x] Losing heat. Cover HHPD can't see into: Sunset Motors' bay always, the bungalow once bought, the car wash once owned. Inside, sightings stop and the trail cools 3× faster, unless a cop is within 28 m. A respray in the bay costs $150 a star (no cop within 45 m); Dee loses a report for $400 a star while she still trusts you; Ammu-Isle's counter takes $250 a star.
  - [x] Cars. A car you bought or already boosted is yours: no heat to get back in. Boosting a stranger's car reports 35 heat, except on your own blocks. The bay parks three cars between sessions. Driving past people on foot at 40 km/h or more sends them diving and reports a little heat.
  - [x] Day and night. Ammu-Isle opens 08:00–22:00; the fronts' night bonus and the extra corner soldier run 20:00–06:00.
- **The city reacts.** Gunfire, assaults, killings and wrecks raise fear where they happen (it settles back in about 30 s). Fear of 35 or more sends residents home and freezes pedestrians, and it shows on the minimap as a red zone. Each hit also moves the owning faction's mood (shown on the phone) and adds heat. Shooting a cop costs 110 heat, drops HHPD's mood by 25 and makes Sgt. Dee stop taking calls. Every reaction lands in the phone's news. When a story job ends, owners, moods and contacts' lines change.
- **Radio.** Three stations play only from a driver's seat (`R` or the Radio touch button steps through them; the last one stays tuned between rides): Neon Tide 94.7 FM (five generated synthwave songs, DJ Luna Vega, jingles), Harbor Talk 1040 AM (The Sully Hour with callers) and Mainsail News Network 88.3 FM, plus four fake ads. Every station keeps running while you are tuned away. Killings, downed cops and gangers, explosions, turf changes, raids, two and four wanted stars, losing the cops and won races become bulletins each station words its own way; at priority 3 (officer down, four stars) MNN breaks in mid-segment. DJs speak through the browser's voices with the line shown on the in-car display, and the radio sits up to 35% under the road at speed, through its own Radio volume.
- **Failure states.** Dying or being arrested drops the live job, and its giver offers it again. A lost raid wrecks a front; a lost block stops paying. Death costs a clinic fee of 8% of cash (minimum $150); arrest costs $150 a star after 1.2 s of a cop inside 2.6 m. A missed courier deadline returns the parcel with no cash penalty, and losing a race is second place, not a wipe. Wanted heat runs to five stars, with cruisers from three.
- **Screen.** The engine's ink kit in Harbor colours: one goal card, one action prompt, vitals, minimap, the filled hotbar slots and a Phone button (`T`). Cargo, wanted stars and the speedometer appear only when they apply.
- **Minute 5 → hour 5.** At 5:00 the first two missions are done with a pistol, about $1,000 and no car. Hour five is cred 3–5 in a bought ground car, the courier loop and bounties as repeatable income, one race circuit learned; aircraft start at $12,000, so they are later than this.
- **References it is chasing.**
  - *GTA: Vice City* — feel: a coast learned by landmark rather than map pin; density: traffic and pedestrians on every block; UI: minimap with mission blips and radio-style notices.
  - *Crazy Taxi* — feel: timer pressure with a clean run always available; density: constant pickups, no downtime; UI: one arrow and one clock, legible while driving.
  - *Need for Speed: Most Wanted* — feel: pursuit that escalates by star; density: street races sharing the same roads; UI: speedometer plus wanted stars readable at a glance.
- **Gap to target.** The rival war now runs without you (raids, pushes, turf), but only one faction fights back, sleeping does not skip time, and fronts have no upgrades. Hour five wants a second rival and a reason to spend the safe beyond more fronts.

Turf proof: `proofs/turf-session.json` (take the Marina, let the furious Carmines push it back to zero and retake it, then start the crew war and watch the Carmines and Riptides push into each other).

Depth proof: `proofs/depth-session.json` (buy a front, take the Marina, pay Dee, hide in the bay, break a raid, collect, let the city hit back overnight, reopen the wrecked front).

Loop proof: to-chase (run with `--state chase`, the game's own `chase.stage`; the flow drives out of the search area and asserts it grew, so the heat ran and the keypress was not the whole event)
