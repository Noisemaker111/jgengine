Original Deepward Fitter and service weapons

The Fitter salvage operator, service sidearm, service rifle, their embedded
material maps, skeleton, animation clips, and source generator
deepward/scripts/build-original-fitter.py are original art authored for Deepward
by the Deepward project contributors. The shared original material and geometry
helpers are in deepward/scripts/build-original-machinery.py. No other game's
art, downloaded character, weapon kit, scanned texture, or third-party mesh is
included. Embedded basecolor/roughness/normal enamel maps and the woven workcoat
map are reproducible authored patterns, not scans. All embedded images are 256px.

To the extent possible under law, the Deepward project contributors have waived
all copyright and related or neighboring rights to these assets, skeleton and
animations, texture maps, and their generator under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Fitter: metres, glTF +Y up, +Z facing, floor-centre origin. The original seventeen
joint skeleton provides idle (2s), walk (1s), and windup (0.7s) clips. Shoulders
have bind pivots (+/-0.36,1.3,0) in glTF space. Rifle and maintenance spanner are
skinned to the hands; neither is a runtime damage/collision authority.

Weapons: metres, glTF +Y up, -Z firing direction, grip origin. The bore centre
matches runtime muzzle anchors (0,0,-0.22) and (0,0,-0.5). The weapons are static
authored props; accepted kick/reload motion remains in the game renderer.

Build: blender -b -t 2 -P deepward/scripts/build-original-fitter.py
Inspect: DEEPWARD_ASSET_SET=fitter blender -b -t 2 -P deepward/scripts/inspect-original-machinery.py

Decoded file hashes, image hashes, bounds, stored triangles, material groups,
and clip/skin metadata are recorded in asset-manifest.json. Offline art previews
do not prove native gameplay integration or locomotion timing.
