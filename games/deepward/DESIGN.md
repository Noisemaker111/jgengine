# Deepward — design

First-person extraction RPG. You live in Marrow, one of the last lit vaults, dive into dead vaults on an oxygen clock, pack salvage into a grid cache, and retrace your route to extract before purge gas kills you.

See [README.md](README.md) for current playable scope and [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) for milestones.

## Core rules

- **No classes; every ability is a hand item.** Guns (ammo), melee (rage), spells (mana), shields/devices (power), and movement (stamina) all compete for hand slots (start with 1 hand, cap at 4).
- **Enemies use the same items and drop what they hold.**
- **Lives end at death.** Extracting keeps everything; dying ends the character, returns a fraction of carried gear/XP to Marrow's stash, and adds printer drift.
- **Oxygen is the dive clock.** Equipment (tank, rings, neck) sets capacity; hand items never extend oxygen.
- **Marrow is permanent.** Homefolk process salvage, upgrade stations, and persist across Lives.
