# Deepward

A municipal salvage shift inside a sealed industrial vault. Marrow's receiving rail is the safe, legible home; Bellwether's corridors feel worked, patched and abandoned. The Fitter is recognizable machinery rather than a fantasy monster.

Use charcoal paneling, worn steel and brass, with cyan rail equipment and amber warnings. Large stenciled signs, visible rail lines and repeated service fixtures orient the player. Warm salvage marks remain distinct from cool route markers. Room lamps reveal usable space without washing away the dark corridor depth.

Architecture, machinery, salvage, weapons and the Fitter rig are original authored assets, consumed through the shared asset catalog and model renderer. Preserve their industrial proportions and silhouettes. Avoid substitute fantasy or generic city packs. HUD panels resemble service paperwork: numbered sections, plain instructions, prominent oxygen and ammunition, and clear permanent-stash versus carried-cache ownership.

Feedback follows the same materials: short mechanical shot and reload bursts, a descending rail cue, a terminal tick and a two-note bank acknowledgement. JGengine owns synthesis, mixing and user audio settings. Return acknowledgement sounds only after the stash is durably saved. Paused menus stop the shift and return focus predictably.
