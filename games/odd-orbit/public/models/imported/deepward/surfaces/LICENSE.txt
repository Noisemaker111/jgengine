Original Deepward station surface maps

All PNG maps in this directory are original authored material maps created for
Deepward by the Deepward project contributors. They are generated reproducibly
by deepward/scripts/build-original-surfaces.py with NumPy and Pillow. No image,
material scan, or texture from a third party or another game is included. These
maps depict authored enamel grain/scuffs and mineral floor grain/grease. They
are not photographed PBR material scans.

To the extent possible under law, the Deepward project contributors have waived
all copyright and related or neighboring rights to these maps and their source
generator under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Each map is tileable, 256x256 pixels, and locally served; dimensions, roles,
color spaces, file sizes, URLs, and SHA256 hashes are in asset-manifest.json.
The color maps are neutral and intended to multiply the editor palette.
Roughness/normal maps are linear; color maps use sRGB. Normal maps use glTF/OpenGL
positive-Y convention. UV coordinates, repeats, and authored surface placement
belong to the game, not this asset generator.
