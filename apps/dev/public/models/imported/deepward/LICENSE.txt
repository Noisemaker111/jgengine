Deepward original industrial machinery

All seven GLB models in this directory and their source generator
deepward/scripts/build-original-machinery.py were authored for Deepward by the
Deepward project contributors. No third-party meshes, image textures, downloaded
models, or other game's assets are included. Geometry, markings, material maps,
and UVs are original. Blender's built-in font is used to construct mesh markings;
no font file is shipped. Blender is an authoring tool and its software license
does not impose a license on generated art.

To the extent possible under law, the Deepward project contributors have waived
all copyright and related or neighboring rights to these model assets and their
generator under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The models use embedded original 256px basecolor/roughness/normal maps plus modeled
surface details. These reproducible authored enamel wear, scratches, and grain
patterns are not photographed scans. They are unrigged, static props. All units are metres,
glTF +Y is up, +Z is the operator-facing side, and each origin is floor centre.
See asset-manifest.json for file hashes, dimensions, and mesh costs.

Rebuild with Blender 4.3 or newer:
blender -b -t 2 -P deepward/scripts/build-original-machinery.py

Optional DEEPWARD_ASSET_EVIDENCE=/absolute/scratch/path writes Blender source
snapshots and studio inspection renders outside shipped assets. Studio renders
are asset inspection evidence, not evidence of integration into the game.
