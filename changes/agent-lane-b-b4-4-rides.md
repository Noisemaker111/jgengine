### Added

- `createRideSystem` (`@jgengine/core/gameplay`) runs a park's rides: each one cycles load → ride → unload at its own capacity, boards from its own line, wears against its own breakdown curve (chance per completed cycle, repair seconds and money) and reports boards, alights, breakdowns and repairs through a sink, so the game keeps the bodies, the visuals and the wallet. `snapshot()`/`restore()` round-trip a park mid-cycle, `retune(id, patch)` changes a ride's ratings, capacity, timings or breakdown curve in play, and `service(id)` pays the maintenance cost and clears wear.
- `createWaitQueue` (`@jgengine/core/gameplay`) is a real line: guests join the back, leave from anywhere, and the wait a new arrival faces comes from the queue's own measured boarding throughput rather than a configured constant. `DEFAULT_QUEUE_SEED_SERVICE_SECONDS` only bootstraps the estimate until two boardings have been observed.
- `createRideChooser`, `rideChoiceOptions`, `rideChoiceCandidates`, `rideQueueCostWeight` and `rideAppeal` (`@jgengine/core/gameplay`) turn a guest's `RideTaste` (intensity comfort, nausea comfort, queue tolerance) plus the park's ride data into `createUtilityBrain` options, each ride costed by the seconds its own line reports — ride choice is one more scored option, not a fixed order.
- `DEFAULT_RIDE_BREAKDOWN`, `DEFAULT_RIDE_RATING_RANGE` and `DEFAULT_RIDE_FUN_CURVE` are the starting curve and scales a park overrides.
