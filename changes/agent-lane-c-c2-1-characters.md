### Added

- `SKELETON_STANDARD`, `mapRigToStandard`, `retargetTracks`, `retargetBoneScales` and the `RIG_SCHEMES` table (`@jgengine/core/anim/skeletonStandard`) are the engine's one humanoid skeleton standard: the CC0 KayKit character rig that `kaykit-adventurers` and `kaykit-skeletons` already share, named slot by slot (`root`, `hips`, `spine`, `chest`, `head`, `upperarm.l`…`toes.r`, `handslot.l/r`). A rig on another naming scheme maps onto those slots through a scheme table — Mixamo (`mixamorig*`) and the Quaternius sci-fi aliens ship as `MIXAMO_SCHEME` and `QUATERNIUS_ALIEN_SCHEME` — and every slot the rig cannot fill comes back in `missing` instead of borrowing a neighbour's bone. `retargetTracks` renames a clip's tracks onto a mapped rig, `retargetBoneScales` gives the per-slot length ratios that match bone proportions, and `runtimeBoneName` is the `.`-stripped spelling the loaded hierarchy and clips both use.
- `locomotionGraph` (`@jgengine/core/anim/locomotionGraph`) now builds the whole directional locomotion set: `strafeLeft`/`strafeRight` add a `strafe` blend on the new `direction` parameter, `backward` adds a `backward` blend behind the new `facing` parameter, and every graph gets a `turn` blend on the new `turnRate` parameter (pivoting on `walk` when no turn clip is authored). Each state joins the idle/walk/run blend through a crossfaded transition, so a direction change blends instead of popping; supplying none of them leaves the speed-only tree unchanged. `LOCOMOTION_DIRECTION_PARAM`, `LOCOMOTION_FACING_PARAM`, `LOCOMOTION_TURN_RATE_PARAM`, `LOCOMOTION_STRAFE_STATE`, `LOCOMOTION_BACKWARD_STATE` and `LOCOMOTION_TURN_STATE` are exported, and a custom mover feeds the three parameters through the entity blackboard key `ANIM_PARAMS_KEY`.
- `ModelAnimationStates` gains `strafeLeft`, `strafeRight`, `backward` and `turn`.
- `classifyClip`/`rolesFromClips` know the `strafe` and `backward` clip roles, so `defaultAnimationForClips` derives the KayKit directional states automatically (`Running_Strafe_Left`, `Running_Strafe_Right`, `Walking_Backwards`) for any rig resolved with `animation: "auto"`.
