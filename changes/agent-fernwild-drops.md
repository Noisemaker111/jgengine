Fernwild now has ARK's supply drops. Crates come down at 38 drop sites spread over the island's biomes, the colour set by place the way ARK does it: white and green on the south beaches and plains, blue in the jungle, redwoods and swamp, purple and yellow in the highlands, yellow and red in the snow and on the volcano. A crate falls for two minutes from 150 m up under a canopy, with a tall beam of its colour to find it by from across the island, and lies twenty minutes once down. Only a survivor of its level opens it (ARK's white 3, green 15, blue 25, purple 35, yellow 45, red 60); a lower one is told the level it needs. Inside are resources, gear, structures and saddles that grow with the colour; white and green use the ARK wiki's resource ranges, the higher tiers are approximate. An emptied or broken crate's site sends the next one 10 to 30 minutes later. A world already under way has crates down from the start.

A fallen survivor's body beam and the crate beams share one engine light-beam piece. The admin `cheat` gains `drop` (send a crate of a colour from the nearest site) and `teleport` takes an optional facing. A new flow, fernwild-drops, shoots a white crate falling, a level 1 survivor turned away, the crate opened at level 3, and a red beam over the snow at night.

Engine seam: packages/core/src/airdrop/airdrop.ts (new), packages/core/src/map/LightBeam.draw.tsx (new)

Prevention: airdrop tests cover one crate per site over an hour of play, a crate falling from its height over its fall time and landing with its tier's loot under the same id, the level gate, a partial take and the next crate between the respawn bounds, expiry, a called drop replacing the site's crate while other sites keep their timers, and save and restore. Fernwild's tests check every crate's loot is a real item, every drop site is on land, the beaches send only white and green and the snow red, crates are down across a fresh world, a crate falls, lands, stays shut at level 1 and empties into the bags at level 3, and a red crate stays shut at level 30.

### Added

- `createAirdropField` in `@jgengine/core/airdrop/airdrop`: timed supply crates at authored sites, a tier picked per site by weight, falling from a height, rolled loot on landing, opened only at the tier's level, expiring when ignored, one per site, with `call` for an admin or scripted drop.
- `LightBeam` in `@jgengine/core/map/LightBeam.draw`: a tall glowing column marking a spot to find from far away.
