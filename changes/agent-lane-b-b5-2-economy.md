### Added

- `createMarket` (`@jgengine/core/gameplay`) prices goods from supply and demand over finite stock on a stated curve: `price = clamp(basePrice × (coverPeriods × demand / max(stock, 1))^elasticity)` between `basePrice × minFactor` and `basePrice × maxFactor`. `sell` decrements the finite stock and refuses a sale it cannot cover with the shortfall named, `restock` lowers the price, `quote`/`priceAfter` read the move without mutating, `recordDemand(unitsSold, unitsUnmet)` folds a period of observed buying into demand, and `priceCurve` replaces the curve. `retune` changes a good's policy in play; `snapshot()`/`restore()` round-trip stock and demand; money is integer minor units.
- `createTransactionLedger` (`@jgengine/core/gameplay`) keeps per-account, per-currency balances in integer minor units with a journal that records every movement, the balance it produced and the reason it happened. `post` is the one money path — sales, purchases, upkeep and loan payments all carry a caller-owned reason and an optional `ref` — and `totals()` keeps a per-reason count and net beyond the `maxEntries` journal cap. `addCharge` registers a recurring charge (upkeep, rent) settled on its cadence by `chargeDue`, bounded per charge and per advance; a cycle with no funds is counted as missed instead of overdrawing.
- `buildLoanSchedule` and `createLoanBook` (`@jgengine/core/gameplay`) draw a loan, post the principal, and settle interest (`"loan-interest"`) and principal (`"loan-principal"`) as separate reason-tagged entries. A schedule is built up front in integer minor units — `"amortized"` levels the payment on the outstanding balance, `"flat"` charges interest on the original principal — and the final payment absorbs the rounding, so the principal parts sum to the principal exactly. A payment that comes due with no funds stays in arrears and counts once per advance; after `maxMissed` the loan is defaulted and stops being advanced. `repay` pays principal early, and state is terms plus progress, so the schedule is rebuilt on restore.
