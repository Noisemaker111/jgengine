Lighting, materials and build rules each live in one `@jgengine/core` folder: `lighting` holds scene lights, cascaded shadows, image-based and probe lighting and the reflection probe; `material` holds the material loaders, authored surfaces, triplanar and scanned-surface shaders and wet surfaces; build sockets and plot permissions join `placement`; decal geometry joins `scene` beside the decal renderer.
