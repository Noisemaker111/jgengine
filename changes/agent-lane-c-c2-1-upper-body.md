### Added

- `actionLayer` and `withActionLayers` (`@jgengine/core/anim/actionLayer`) build a masked overlay layer from plain data: a resting clip plus a one-shot state per entry in `actions`, each joined by a consumed-trigger transition and returning on `exitTime`. Aim, reload, wave and hit reactions are one builder instead of a hand-written state machine per game. `additive: true` makes it a recoil/breathing overlay — the shell's `makeClipAdditive` makes the rest clip's first frame the zero reference, so the rest state is held at `speed: 0` and contributes no pose while the base keeps animating. `mask` is required and nonempty, because an overlay that names no bones would drive the whole rig; `withActionLayers` rejects a duplicate layer id instead of shadowing the base.
- `standardBoneMask`, `UPPER_BODY_SLOTS`/`LOWER_BODY_SLOTS` and `UPPER_BODY_MASK`/`LOWER_BODY_MASK` (`@jgengine/core/anim/skeletonStandard`) derive a layer mask from the skeleton standard's own slots, so a rig mapped onto the standard is masked without hand-typing its bone names.
- `ModelLookAtConfig` (`@jgengine/core/game/playableGame`, `ModelConfig.ik.lookAt`) can aim at more than the camera: `target` takes `"camera"` (default), `{ entity }` offset by `entityEyeHeight` (default 1.5), or `{ point }`. `maxYaw`/`maxPitch` bound the turn in radians and `weight` (default 1) scales how much of the solved turn reaches the bone. An entity target that is not spawned keeps its last known direction instead of snapping the head back to the camera. `applyFootIk`'s sixth argument is now `lookAtTarget` and applies the limits and weight; `FootIkRig` carries `lookAtLimits` and `lookAtWeight`.
- The shell derives the directional locomotion parameters from the entity's own movement, so a rig with the `strafe`/`backward` states uses them with no game-side code: `direction` (the motion's share along the character's right axis), `facing` (its share along the facing axis) and `turnRate` (its yaw change per second) come from the entity's `position` and `rotationY` each visual sample and override conflicting authored extras. `readModelAnimationParams` gains an optional `ModelMotionStep` argument (`dx`, `dz`, `dyaw`, `seconds`); omitting it leaves direction to the authored extras, which is the previous behaviour. A step past the live `snapDistance` reports no direction and zero speed rather than inventing a lateral.
