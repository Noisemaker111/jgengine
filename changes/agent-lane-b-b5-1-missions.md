### Added

- `createMissionRuntime` (`@jgengine/core/gameplay`) runs missions as data: a mission is a graph of objectives the machine walks, with named fail states, per-objective timers, conditional branches, per-objective and completion rewards, a `rollout` fact rolled from the injected RNG, and a `next` mission that chains a follow-up. `tick(dt, world)` advances the active mission against caller-supplied actors, counters, facts and survivors; `signal`/`fail` let an authored trigger complete or fail it. `snapshot()`/`restore()` round-trip a run, `stats()` and an optional `devtoolsName` probe expose it, and cost per tick is the active objective's own fail list plus one goal test.
- Objective kinds are the shared primitives `reach`, `interact`, `survive`, `pursue`, `kill` and `collect`; a fail condition can be `actor-died`, `timer` or `fact`.
- `escortMission`, `chaseMission` and `defendMission` (`@jgengine/core/gameplay`) are compositions of those kinds, not archetypes: an escort is a `reach` with an `actor-died` guard, a defend is a `survive` with a survivor floor that banks what is left standing as the score, and a chase is a `pursue` with an escape guard.
- `validateMissionCatalog` (`@jgengine/core/gameplay`) is the mission content gate: duplicate ids, unknown branch/next/timeout targets, unknown fail states and markers, per-kind required fields, unreachable objectives, and the counter-only rule — a mission with a completion path made entirely of `kill`/`collect` objectives is an error. `createMissionRuntime` runs it at construction, so a counter-only mission throws instead of shipping.
- `registerMissionTriggerActions`, `MISSION_TRIGGER_ACTIONS`, `createMissionTriggerBridge` and `missionMarkers` (`@jgengine/core/gameplay`) start, advance and fail a mission from authored scene markers using the shared `on`/`action` trigger vocabulary; positional objectives read marker positions off the authored document.
- `applyMissionRewards` (`@jgengine/core/gameplay`) pays a mission through the same appliers quests use (XP, currency, items, unlocks) and rolls named loot tables through the game's loot registry.
