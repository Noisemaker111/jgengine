### Fixed

- grass no longer reads as a spotted patch. Tuft roots are laid on an even low-discrepancy pattern instead of random scatter, which left piles and bare gaps, and they stay even when distance thins them.
- each tuft's blades now spread far enough to meet the next tuft (at least three quarters of the tuft spacing), so a field is one sward instead of fountain islands on the turf mat.
- grass blades seen from behind are no longer near black. three.js flipped the whole normal on back faces and pointed the blade's sky lift into the ground. Both sides of a blade now light from above, and the lower blade sits in an even shade so a dense field still reads deep.
- clumping only pulls tufts into clumps when a look asks for strong clumping (or cushions), so ordinary lawns and meadows stop forming bald patches.
