### Added

- seeded nature generators for broadleaf, conifer, palm, bush, fern, rock and driftwood, with presets for oak, birch, tropical, dead tree, pine, spruce, redwood, coconut, cycad, fan palm, shrub, berry bush, hedge, fern, tree-fern, bracken, rock, boulder, pebbles, slab and fallen log
- a scatter palette item can grow from a generator ({ generator, preset, params, variants }); its variants are baked once, instanced, and share one far impostor
- editor markers can place any generator family that registers a mesh builder, not only parts generators
- a nature variant grid demo in apps/dev (nature-variants)
- `getGeneratorMesh` (`@jgengine/core/generator/generatorMesh.draw`): The mesh builder registered for an output family, or undefined. Also `GeneratorMeshBuilder`, `GeneratorMeshSource`, `registerGeneratorMesh`.
- `GeneratorMeshInstance` (`@jgengine/core/generator/GeneratorMeshInstance.draw`): One placed generator instance drawn through the mesh builder registered for its output family (`registerGeneratorMesh`), regrown from its params and seed. Also `GeneratorMeshInstanceProps`.
- `BROADLEAF_GENERATOR` (`@jgengine/core/nature/natureGenerators`): Broadleaf trees: oak, birch, tropical hardwood, dead tree. Also `BROADLEAF_SCHEMA`, `BUSH_GENERATOR`, `BUSH_SCHEMA`, `CONIFER_GENERATOR`, `CONIFER_SCHEMA`, `DRIFTWOOD_GENERATOR`, `DRIFTWOOD_SCHEMA`, `FERN_GENERATOR` and 7 more.
- `addBlob` (`@jgengine/core/nature/natureMesh`): A lumpy ellipsoid: a leaf cluster, a bush clump, a coconut. Also `BlobOptions`, `ConeOptions`, `FrondOptions`, `NatureMesh`, `NatureRgb`, `NatureVec`, `StoneOptions`, `TubeOptions` and 5 more.
- `natureGeometry` (`@jgengine/core/nature/natureMesh.draw`): A three geometry for a nature mesh, its sRGB vertex colours converted to linear. Also `natureMaterial`.
- `isScatterGeneratorItem` (`@jgengine/core/scatter/scatterGenerator`): True when a scatter mapping names a generator rather than a model. Also `ScatterGeneratorItem`, `scatterGeneratorBase`, `scatterGeneratorVariantCount`.
- `placedGeneratorFor` (`@jgengine/core/scene/assetGenerator`): The placeable generator (parts, nature …) a placed marker's `meta.assetId` names, or undefined. Also `registerPlaceableGeneratorOutput`.
- `BROADLEAF_GENERATOR` (`@jgengine/core/world`): Broadleaf trees: oak, birch, tropical hardwood, dead tree. Also `BROADLEAF_SCHEMA`, `BUSH_GENERATOR`, `BUSH_SCHEMA`, `BlobOptions`, `CONIFER_GENERATOR`, `CONIFER_SCHEMA`, `ConeOptions`, `DRIFTWOOD_GENERATOR` and 27 more.
