### Added

- 46 older CC0 Quaternius packs from OpenGameArt (1,462 models: characters, animals, monsters, vehicles, buildings, weapons, food, nature) are in the asset library with triangles, bounds and clips; the Fantasy Props MegaKit is pinned to its CC0 OpenGameArt zip (94 models). Re-uploads by other users and the CC-BY-SA 2016 packs are left out.
- Pack pulls convert FBX- and OBJ-only archives to GLB (`extractPackModels`, `@jgengine/assets/packModelConvert`): rig and clips kept, centimetres made metres, solid materials kept opaque, one draw per material, colour textures attached by file or by material name.
