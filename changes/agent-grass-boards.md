Grass reference boards: photos beside our views, stylised targets, game views and species

### Added

- Grass reference boards: `bun run shoot --board` shoots six credited photos beside our matching views, our games' stylised art beside our grass, the 18 grass game views and every species, with `--diff` to compare two boards and `--resume`/`--timeout` to finish a long board
- `--look-from` takes a fourth number, the ground clearance, so a capture can sit centimetres above the turf
