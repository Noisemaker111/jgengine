### Added

- `ragdollConfigFromPoses`, `ragdollBoneOrientations`, `RagdollPose`, `RagdollBodyShape` and `HUMANOID_RAGDOLL_BODIES` (`@jgengine/core/anim/ragdollRig`) turn a rig mapped onto the skeleton standard into a physics ragdoll at the moment of death: one box body per standard slot that has a shape, sized to the character, and one distance link per body to its nearest shaped ancestor, holding the bones' own separation. The bodies start where the character was, so the collapse continues the death pose. `handSlot.l/r` carry no body (prop sockets, not body parts); a slot the rig lacks is skipped and its children link to the nearest present ancestor. Because the engine's `PhysicsWorld` bodies carry translation only, `ragdollBoneOrientations` gives the skeleton its orientation by rotating each bone's death-pose direction-to-child onto the current one.
- `useRagdoll` (`@jgengine/shell/render/useRagdoll`) arms a ragdoll on a rigged character when `active` goes true and drives every mapped bone's world position and aim from its body each frame. It steps the injected `PhysicsWorld`; deactivating removes the bodies and leaves the rig where the ragdoll put it. Stop the character's `ModelConfig.ik` while it is active, or the two write the same joints.
- `multiplyQuaternions` and `quaternionFromUnitVectors` (`@jgengine/core/anim/ikSolver`) are the two pure rotation helpers the ragdoll's aim needs.
- `locomotionGraph({ rootMotion: true })` (and `ModelAnimationStates.rootMotion`) marks the whole locomotion set — idle/walk/run, strafe, backward and turn — as root-motion states, so one authored locomotion graph carries its own travel as `AnimGraphOutput.rootDelta` for the movement owner to consume.
