### Added

- `resolveRoutine` (`@jgengine/core/gameplay`) resolves an authored day into real arrival and departure times against the world clock. Travel time is injected per stop or as a callback, so a stop departs early enough to walk to the next one and an arrival that runs late slips instead of teleporting; `phaseAt(time)` returns the held place, the walking leg and its progress, and `nextChangeAfter(time)` drives a scheduler. A day whose walks cannot fit its cycle throws at construction.
- `createOffScreenWorld` (`@jgengine/core/gameplay`) keeps a whole population without simulating it: residents beyond the injected radius are cheap state (position, routine phase, needs) that only moves when the clock crosses a routine boundary, and they spawn into the game's own near simulation through a `NearAgents` interface when they come close. The radius, an optional per-agent cost model, a near budget and a per-tick switch budget are all injected; `snapshot`/`restore` round-trip the whole population, and crossing the boundary preserves identity, schedule progress, need values and position.
- `createNearAgents` (`@jgengine/core/gameplay`) is the default near half of that seam: a nav crowd with a need set and utility brain per agent, goal-tracking as routines move residents between places, and a decision counter for tests and probes.
