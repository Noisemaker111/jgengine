### Added

- `createDamageRuntime` (`@jgengine/core/combat`) composes a damage table, hit zones, the armor curve and carried statuses into one stateful model: `hit()` returns every stage of the number (`base × zone multiplier × type multiplier × (1 − armor mitigation)`), `tick()` runs status effects through the same table and armor, `timeToKill()` quotes a fight, and `table()` reads the whole chart. `snapshot()`/`restore()` carry registered targets and carried statuses; `retune()` swaps table, zones or status definitions without dropping either; a `devtoolsName` probe reports hits, crits, ticks and the last hit.
- A damage table (`combat/damageTable`) of damage type × resistance class, with `armor / (armor + halfValue)` mitigation — armor equal to the half value halves the hit — a per-type `bypassArmor`, and `readDamageTable` / `formatDamageTable` to read every row at given armor values. An unknown damage type throws instead of silently dealing 1×.
- Hit zones and crits (`combat/hitZones`): a zone carries a normal multiplier and an optional crit multiplier, `resolveHitZone` rolls once and returns one multiplier, and a crit replaces the zone multiplier rather than compounding with it. `hitZoneMultiplier` quotes the same number without a roll.
- `createStatusRuntime` (`combat/statusRuntime`): data-defined statuses with the existing `StatusStackPolicy` (`refresh`, stack to `max`, `replace`, `ignore`) as the only thing that changes stacks, per-tick effects of `{ kind: "damage", channel }` or `{ kind: "stat", stat }`, damage routed through an injected `applyDamage` so resistances and armor still apply, bounded ticks per call, and plain-data state out and back.
- Numbers from the tests: 25 base kinetic against a 150 HP target, 0.15 s between shots — 8 shots / 1.05 s unarmored, 20 shots / 2.85 s at 60 armor, 36 shots / 5.25 s at 200 armor; two stacked burns of 4 per tick deal 8, not 16; a 1.5× head zone with a 2.5× crit multiplier deals 2.5×, not 3.75×.
