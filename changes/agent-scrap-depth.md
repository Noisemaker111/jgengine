Scrap Signal plays its first ten minutes the Borderlands 2 way, proven from a new game, and is off the depth known-failing list

Engine seam: none - game content only

### Added

- A from-zero proof plays a new Scrap Signal game for ten minutes through real input: Bolt's quests one at a time, the crash chest's pistol, the whelps, the convoy's bruisers and red crate, Old Tusk and his whelps, the road to Breakwater Shelf and Captain Rusk. Scrap Signal is off the depth known-failing list for both from-zero and depth.
- Camp enemies carry four times their listed health and shield (one dial); bosses and Overdrive crews keep their own numbers; cleared camps refill after four minutes away.
- Ammo crates that restock, ammo packs sized to a camp fight, and kills that always drop ammo while you are nearly dry.
- Named targets behind side quests and quest-reward uniques.

### Fixed

- Rockets burst on the first thing they hit instead of flying through it.
- Chest and crate drops land two metres out on the opener's side, so picking one up no longer puts the camera inside the chest.

### Changed

- The Rustflat Front's crews wake only after Old Tusk is down; Old Tusk has 2000 health, Captain Rusk 1800 plus a 400 shield.
- Bruiser Bash now ends at the convoy's red crate, and Old Tusk's quest starts by thinning his whelps.
