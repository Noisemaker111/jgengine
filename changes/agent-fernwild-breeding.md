Fernwild now has ARK's breeding. Every creature is born male or female with its wild levels spread over its stats. A tamed female and male of a kind with mating on, left side by side, mate for about a minute; she lays a fertilized egg or carries her young on the ARK wiki's incubation, gestation and maturation times for every species that breeds. An egg hatches only inside its kind's temperature band, read from the island's climate where it lies, so a campfire a few metres off keeps a raptor egg going at night. The baby hatches at a quarter size with each stat whole from one parent (55% the higher) and a 2.5% chance per roll of a two-level mutation, sometimes as twins or triplets. It eats from its own inventory, grows to full size, and every eight hours asks for a cuddle, a walk or food; each answered request adds imprint, which raises its health, food, weight and melee. The tame panel shows the mating switch, the mating and gestation meters and the cooldown, and for a baby its stage, food, imprint and what it wants. `cheat breedingSpeed` stands in for ARK's server multipliers, and `summon` takes a sex, tamed and mating.

Every animated character drawn on its own rig now stays inside its body. The scene's culled-matrix pass recomputed world matrices but never refreshed an attached skin's bind inverse, so a skinned creature was skinned against wherever it was first mounted: a raptor or dodo standing beside the survivor drew a kilometre away, and walkers drifted off their bodies. Fernwild's creatures near the camera were invisible before this.

Prevention: breeding tests cover a pair mating only in range and resetting when parted, an egg progressing only inside its temperature and dying outside it, gestation and birth, the baby stages, care requests and the imprint per answered request, speeds and save and restore, twins and triplets, the 55% inheritance and mutations, and spreading wild levels. Fernwild's tests check the wiki times and the sterile species, and drive a raptor pair from mating to a cold egg, a campfire bringing it into range, a hatch with inherited stats, a starving baby fed meat, a care request answered and its imprint lifting its health, to full size; and a phiomia's pregnancy and birth. A culled-matrix test fails when a moved skinned mesh's skin stays where it was mounted. The fernwild-breed flow shoots the pair mating, the egg by the fire, the hatchling and the growing juvenile.

Bends: `survivor.test.ts`'s punch loop now swings until it has 10 thatch and 1 wood (up to 30 swings) and checks thatch outnumbers wood two to one, instead of a fixed swing count. Creatures now draw extra random numbers at spawn (sex and stat spread), which shifted the random stream that test leaned on; the bias it checks (fists strip thatch far faster than wood) is still asserted.

### Added

- `createBreedingField` in `@jgengine/core/breeding/breeding`: mating pairs in range, eggs incubating inside a temperature band or gestation, hatching or birth, baby stages, care requests and imprinting, multiples, cooldowns, speed multipliers and save/restore; the game supplies species times and how genes combine.
- `inheritStats` and `scatterLevels` in `@jgengine/core/breeding/inheritance`: per-stat inheritance from two parents with mutation rolls, and spreading levels over stats at random.
- `SceneEntity.size`: one entity's model drawn larger or smaller than its kind's scale, on its own rig and in a crowd batch, so a hatchling grows up without a new kind.

### Fixed
