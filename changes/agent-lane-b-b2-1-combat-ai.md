### Added

- `createCoverField` and `coverPointsFromSolids` (`@jgengine/core/combat`) derive stand-able cover from authored solid boxes instead of a coordinate list: one row of points per vertical face, clear of the corners, filtered by an injected `walkable` test, rebuilt when the world is edited, and queried through a bounded `near(position, radius, limit)`. `CoverSource` is the seam, so a game with its own spatial index can supply cover without the box generator.
- `selectCover` / `scoreCoverPoint` choose cover by threat: `hidden` is the confidence-weighted share of remembered threats whose line of sight the point (and a body-sized footprint at it) blocks, scored against the role's preferred fighting range, flank angle around the threat, distance, a squadmate's claim on the point, and whether the walk to it is a detour. Fading memories stop steering at `minConfidence`, and a point that hides from only one of two shooters is not cover.
- `createSquadCombat` (`@jgengine/core/combat`) drives a squad over existing seams: role-driven cover selection, fire discipline (hold, lean out, shoot), suppression from `underFire()`, retreat below a role's health threshold, and relocation under sustained fire. Threat comes only from `threatsFromMemory`, i.e. the perception memory the game already keeps; movement goes through `NavCrowd` with a tighter arrival tolerance so a body actually reaches the stand point. Serializable through `snapshot()`/`restore()` (member decisions, crowd, tuning), retunable, and a `devtoolsName` probe reports pins, peeks, shots and the worst exposure.
- `assignSquadRoles` and `threatsFromMemory` (`@jgengine/core/combat`): deterministic role assignment over a caller-authored `SquadComposition` (role ids, counts, ranges, flank angles, retreat thresholds and cover weights are all content), and a bounded, confidence-ranked threat list from one observer's memory.
- A 2v4 arena soak proves the behavior: 90 simulated seconds, 2700 ticks, 4 squad members against 2 scripted raiders, worst exposure 2.33 s (bar 3 s), 0 of 38 exposure spells over 3 s, per-tick cost p50 0.05 ms / p95 0.19 ms.
