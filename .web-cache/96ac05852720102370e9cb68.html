You can check all the base Creature statistics for  ARK: Survival Evolved here.

  • +W is the increase per level in the wild
  • +T is the increase per level domesticated / tamed in percent of the value when the creature was just tamed

For example, if an Ankylosaurus has 2000 health points after taming, the increase per level is 124 (6.2% of 2000).

Creatures with two attacks (e.g. Beelzebufo or Pteranodon) have two damage values separated by a slash. For the Titanomyrma the values are given for the drone and the soldier.

Untameable creatures are distinguished by a red tint on their name.
